The Noir Tusser Silk Jal is a quiet statement — a black ground worked over with an all-over copper zari jaal that reveals itself only as the light moves. Each saree takes several weeks on the handloom, and the slight irregularities in the weave are the signature of the human hand behind it.

Pair it with an antique-gold blouse for festive evenings, or keep it minimal with oxidised silver for a contemporary look.

Fabric

Pure Tusser (wild) silk, 5.5m with an 0.8m running blouse piece. Handwoven jaal with copper zari.

Care

Dry clean only. Store folded in a cotton or muslin wrap, away from direct sunlight. Refold along different lines every few months to avoid permanent creasing.

Shipping

Dispatched in 2–3 working days; delivered in 4–7. Complimentary shipping on orders over ₹5,000. Cash on delivery available across India.

Returns

7-day easy returns and exchange on unworn pieces with tags intact. See our returns and exchange policies.
