Saree care

Handwoven textiles reward a little care. Here's how to keep every drape beautiful.

Cleaning

  • Silk & Tusser: Dry clean only, especially for the first wash.
  • Cotton & Khadi: Gentle hand wash in cold water with a mild detergent; wash separately the first few times as natural dyes may bleed.
  • Never wring. Roll in a towel to remove excess water and dry in shade.

Storing

  • Wrap in a cotton or muslin cloth — never plastic, which traps moisture.
  • Refold along different lines every couple of months to prevent permanent creases along the zari.
  • Keep away from direct sunlight to protect natural dyes.

Ironing

Use a medium iron on the reverse. For zari borders, place a thin cotton cloth between the iron and the saree.

A handwoven saree carries small irregularities — a slightly uneven weave, a knot in the yarn. These are not flaws; they are the fingerprint of the hand that made it.
