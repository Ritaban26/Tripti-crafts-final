Getting it to you

Shipping

How and when your saree reaches you, wherever you are in the world.

Dispatch & delivery times

Orders are dispatched within 2–3 working days. Once shipped, delivery timelines are:

  • Within India: 4–7 working days
  • International: 7–14 working days

Shipping charges

Complimentary shipping on all Indian orders over ₹5,000. Below that, a flat ₹99 applies. International shipping is calculated at checkout based on destination and weight.

Cash on delivery

COD is available across India on orders up to ₹20,000. A small handling fee may apply.

Tracking

You'll receive a tracking link by email and SMS as soon as your order ships. You can also track your order here at any time.

Because each saree is handwoven and often one of a kind, we pack every parcel by hand with care. If your region is remote, delivery may take a little longer — we'll always keep you informed.
