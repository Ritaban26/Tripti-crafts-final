Our story

Tripti means satisfaction.

The name captures the essence of our late mother — a woman of fierce energy, unshakable conviction and unwavering persistence. This initiative is our heartfelt way of honouring her legacy.

Through Tripti, we celebrate strength, values and resilience — carrying her spirit forward in every handwoven saree.
